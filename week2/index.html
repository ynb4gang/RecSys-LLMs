Scoring Formula

Recommendations are ranked by cosine similarity only: cos(a, b) = dot(a, b) / (||a|| * ||b||), where a and b are 18-dimensional binary genre vectors (or the averaged profile vector). Both vector norms are used; if either norm is zero the similarity is defined as 0.

Popularity (rating count derived from u.data) is evaluation metadata only — it is not part of the ranking score. Cosine normalization removes the direct vector-magnitude advantage that a raw dot product gives multi-genre (often mainstream) titles, but it does not guarantee removal of mainstream/popularity bias: popular titles often carry broad, generic genre blends that can still achieve high cosine similarity.
