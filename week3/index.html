Why support weighting?

For a single positive co-rating, raw cosine is 1.0 regardless of rating magnitude — a user who rated a movie 1 and another who rated it 5 look perfectly alike. So we require at least 3 common ratings and shrink similarity by n / (n + 10), which is near zero on a coincidence and approaches one as evidence accumulates.
